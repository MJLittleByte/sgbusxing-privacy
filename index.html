A neat, no-login Singapore bus companion for Android.

  • Next 3 buses at any stop, with crowd level and single/double deck
  • Save your favourite stops, no account needed
  • The full route of any bus service
  • Nearby bus stops on a map
  • MRT service status at a glance
  • Rain near you

Privacy

No account needed, and your location stays on your phone. The app is free; a future version will show ads to keep it that way. Read the full Privacy Policy.
